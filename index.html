Welcome to FitCheck AR

This app lets you place a true-to-scale 3D bounding box of large items—furniture, appliances, packages—directly into your physical space using your phone's camera.

🔍

Step 1 – Scan the Floor

After tapping Enter AR, slowly move your phone side-to-side, pointing at the floor. A cyan reticle ring will appear when a flat surface is detected.

👆

Step 2 – Place the Box

Tap anywhere on the screen to anchor the bounding box at the reticle's position. The box appears in green when clear, and turns red if a collision is detected.

🕹️

Step 3 – Fine-Tune Position

Use the D-pad to nudge the box precisely. Rotate with ↺↻, adjust height with ↑↓, and toggle Fine (2 cm) / Coarse (10 cm) step sizes.
